# Data requirements for ALLO-FM and GvHD-Bench v1.0 — one institution

Generated by `ALLO_FM_GvHD_foundation_model.ipynb` on 2026-10-26. Every number is computed in the notebook (section 9). The plan assumes **one large transplant centre**: about 200 transplants a year, **at most 1,500 patients** with an EHR timeline and 420 with banked specimens. Items marked † are planning assumptions. No costs are estimated.

## 1 Headline numbers

- **Show a gain**: +0.03 AUROC over a correlated baseline needs about 836 patients (score correlation 0.85); +0.05 needs 299; +0.02 needs 1,893, beyond one centre.
- **Head size**: 1,500 patients support a head of about 24 parameters (AUROC 0.72, prevalence 0.35); the 900 patients of a 60/40 temporal development set about 14; the 420 with specimens about 6.
- **Model**: a tiny timeline model from scratch is about 568k parameters (5 s on one A100); the preferred route is a released pretrained model kept frozen with a head of 5–25 parameters (embeddings for the cohort in 34 s).
- **Tasks runnable at one institution**: 12 of 25 numeric-target tasks (A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, B1, B6, C3, D4, E1, E2, E4); 9 estimable only (B2, B3, B4, B5, C1, C2, C5, C6, D3); 4 out of reach (C4, C7, D1, E3).
- **Transcriptomes at diagnosis**: a pre-specified tissue score is validated on 60–120 biopsies (120 are collected in three years); a 10-parameter adapter head *learns* tissue differential diagnosis from 34 profiles but showing +0.05 over the clinical baseline takes 241 (210 eligible, marginal); steroid refractoriness, the day-7 blood transcriptome and any whole-transcriptome head are not feasible at one centre.
- **Longitudinal**: 6 draws per window at single-draw reliability 0.5; draws 2–3.5 days apart for a 10-day prodrome; about 297 patients with weekly draws for 100 evaluable cases.
- **Level-1C gate**: passed with probability 89% for a true gain of +0.05 on 600 temporal-test patients; about 2% for +0.01.

## 8 Assumptions (†)

- 200 transplants a year; 7.5 years of EHR timelines; 3 years of banked specimens at 70% usable; about 20 % of transplants biopsied for suspected GvHD.
- Eligible fraction of transplants per task (§8.4); onset of grade II–IV aGvHD log-normal with median day 28; pre-onset window 14 days; subgroup shares as listed in section 6.
- Retained-signal fraction κ of a frozen adapter = 0.9 (to be measured by tasks F2 and G4); the effective dimension of a raw-gene head is inflated by the nuisance multiplier measured in §9.4.
