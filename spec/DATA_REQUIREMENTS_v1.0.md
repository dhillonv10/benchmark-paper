# Data requirements for ALLO-FM and GvHD-Bench v1.0

Generated by `ALLO_FM_GvHD_foundation_model.ipynb` on 2026-10-26. Every number below is computed in the notebook (section 9); items marked † are planning assumptions (unit costs, subgroup shares, onset-time distribution, retained-signal fraction kappa).

## 1 Headline requirements

- **Paired comparison**: showing a gain of +0.03 AUROC over a correlated baseline needs about 836 patients (score correlation 0.85; 2,125 at 0.60).
- **Development**: a 768-parameter head at AUROC 0.72 and prevalence 0.35 needs 47,662 patients (Riley); a 50-parameter head 3,103.
- **Transcriptome at diagnosis**: a whole-transcriptome head needs 387,913-1,018,604 labelled profiles to come within 0.02 AUROC of its ceiling; a 64-d adapter embedding retaining 90% of the signal needs 678-1,698.
- **Centres**: 17 centres of ~300 patients give the gate 80% power for a +0.03 gain at between-centre SD 0.02; 40 at 0.04.
- **Longitudinal**: with single-draw reliability 0.5, 6 draws per window recover the oracle AUROC to within 0.02; sampling interval must be well under the prodrome length (every 2-3.5 days for a 10-day prodrome).
- **Validation (Level 3)**: 1,117 patients (168 events) at AUROC 0.75 and prevalence 0.15.
- **Compute**: the whole training programme is about 95 h on one A100 ($191); data collection and assays cost orders of magnitude more.
