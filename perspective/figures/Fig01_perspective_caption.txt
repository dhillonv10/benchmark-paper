Is a foundation model for GvHD plausible? A check on the one public longitudinal cohort (one centre, 584 adults, 93 with severe aGvHD; daily labs, vitals and fluid loss), with every block predicted from earlier patients only. (A) Severe aGvHD starts between day 16 and day 90 (shaded: middle half); blood counts, chemistry, vitals and fluid loss are dense through that window, flow cytometry and cytokines are not. (B) Change in AUROC (same-day pairs; dotted line: the +0.01 margin of the gate) for forecasting severe onset in the next 14 days, against baseline covariates plus time and known aGvHD: a head that takes the whole recent window (615 inputs) loses 0.10; single modalities add nothing detectable; a 5-dimensional label-free summary gains +0.017 (95% CI -0.003 to +0.036), which sits inside the spread of the same arm when the outcome is shuffled (grey bar: central 95% of 20 shuffles; P = 0.14). (C) EHRSHOT-style few-shot curves (mean of three blocks and 25 draws): label-free summaries make the timeline harmless, not helpful; the whole window is harmful. (D) Chance that a gate fixed in advance (interval lower bound at least +0.01) is passed at the observed standard error, by cohort size: the observed gain would pass 11% of the time at this cohort size and 27% at 1,500 patients.
